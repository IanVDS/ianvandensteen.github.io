About

I'm a third-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo. I care about systems that sit at the intersection of mechanical design, electronics, and firmware — especially when they have to move through the world reliably.

Most recently I co-oped at Vermeer, working on GPS-alternative navigation for drones. On campus I'm a member of the Waterloo Aerial Robotics Group (WARG), where I help build and fly autonomous aircraft.
